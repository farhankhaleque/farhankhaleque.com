About

I'm studying Computer Science and Middle Eastern Studies at NYU, and I graduate in May 2027.

Most of what I know about fashion I learned by doing it. I started as a pattern maker at Lusaka Group, then designed for Pull&Bear and spent a lot of time in factories and wash facilities around Bangladesh. At Street Denim I went from checking samples to managing wholesale accounts. This past summer I was on the men's designer shoe team at Bloomingdale's.

The coding side snuck in because the work kept asking for it. A stock check that took hours every day turned into a script. A store that needed 300 styles uploaded turned into a Shopify build. Now I like to sit right where those two things meet.
